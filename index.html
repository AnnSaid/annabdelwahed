How I work

Every analysis follows the same path, so the result is something a manager can act on.

Ask

I start with the decision someone has to make, not with the data.

Analyse

I clean, model and test the data until the answer is reliable.

Decide

I present one clear recommendation, with the visuals that support it.

Training is part of the job

I have delivered full data analysis courses of 60 and 80 hours, covering Excel, SQL, Tableau and Power BI. Explaining a method clearly makes me a better analyst, and it means I can bring a whole team along.
